# Privacy and export

Before sharing a learning with a team, classify it:

- `personal`: useful to the individual, not meant for team sharing
- `team-shareable`: safe to share after review
- `sensitive`: may include internal context and needs careful redaction
- `do-not-share`: should not be exported

Team-safe exports should remove:

- credentials, tokens, keys, and secrets
- private file paths
- customer data
- incident details
- sensitive internal URLs
- unnecessary personal names
- confidential project details

Prefer a short, principle-focused export over a detailed incident narrative.
